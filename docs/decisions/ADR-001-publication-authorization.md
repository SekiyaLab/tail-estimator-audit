# ADR-001: Publication authorization

## Status

Accepted — 2026-09-15

## Context

The study went through two review rounds:

- `docs/reviews/2026-09-02-75714e8-forensic-review.md` — **REVISE**, on a narrow
  GPD-POT denominator/failure-rate reporting correction. The scientific
  contract and candidate results were not in question.
- `docs/reviews/2026-09-02-9360aa0-r01-confirmation.md` — the correction was
  applied and independently reconfirmed **ACCEPT**: "the local Track B
  history through this confirmation is accepted as public-ready pending a
  separate explicit publication decision."

Both review rounds are part of the permanent scientific record and are
preserved unedited by this decision — the REVISE is not a defect to hide;
it is evidence the review process works.

## Decision

Joshua authorized publication on 2026-09-15. The repository
(`SekiyaLab/tail-estimator-audit`) is made public on GitHub, and its
`sekiyalab.github.io` research-index listing is updated from `Private` to
`Public`.

## What did not change

No scientific content, results, methodology, review history, or accepted
artifact was altered by this decision. Neither review file is edited. No
prior "publication not authorized" statement existed in `README.md` or
`docs/research_contract.md` to correct — the gate lived only in the reviews
themselves, which remain visible exactly as written.

## Plain-language model

- **What this does:** turns an already-accepted, already-verified study from
  private to public.
- **Why it matters:** the study cleared its evidence bar on 2026-09-02
  (after one documentation-only correction cycle); the estate held it
  privately gated for over a week on authorization alone.
- **What fails if it is incorrect:** none of the science is affected — this
  is a visibility change, not a re-review.
- **What remains uncertain:** nothing about this study; general publication
  cadence for other accepted-but-private studies remains a separate future
  decision.
